Character Sheet panel — the real module, mounted outside ComfyUI

This page imports web/js/h3sheet_core.mjs itself and mounts the panel with a fake wiring layer, exactly the way the node does. What you see is the pack's actual panel — the new card rails, the glyph tick rows, the resolution chips — not a drawing of it. Buttons that would talk to ComfyUI (render, upload, blur) are stubbed and log to the console at the bottom - which is why Render is disabled here: it presses ComfyUI's own queue, and there is no queue on this page. The two layout cards wearing a sheet with a yours badge show a placeholder for the card art: the real node fills those from your own last render of that layout, and every layout you have not rendered keeps the drawn arrangement.
